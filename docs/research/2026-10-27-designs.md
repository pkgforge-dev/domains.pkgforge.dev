# The two designs, side by side

Date: 2026-10-27. Two agents designed `domains.pkgforge.dev` alone, each with its own measurements.
Design A is `2026-10-27-design-a.md`. Design B is `2026-10-27-design-b.md`.
This file records where they agree, where they split, what decided each split, and what each corrected in the older research.
`docs/architecture.md` is the design in force.

The operator's rulings of 2026-10-27 (`TODO/RULES.md` §5) came after both designs. Where a ruling overturned a design, this file says so.

## 1. Where they agree

Both designs reached the same answers without seeing each other's work. These are adopted.

| Point | What both say | Where it is adopted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Writers and readers | GitHub Actions writes. GHCR is the only copy of the truth, with write-once dated tags and a root that moves forward. Workers read anonymously with `Authorization: Bearer QQ==`, follow the blob's 307 to an Azure signed URL, and read it with `Range`. R2 is a cache that can be rebuilt, with the Cache API in front. | `docs/architecture.md` §4, §4.7, §4.9 |
| Data format | Immutable sorted packs, front-coded and block-compressed, with a sparse index. An observation is a name, a source, a first and a last time, and a precision. A merge by minimum and maximum is idempotent. | §3.3, §3.4, §4.4 |
| No source is complete | Take the union. Every answer lists the sources used and the sources that failed. Publish a coverage table daily. | §8, §9 |
| Definitions | `seen`, `claimed_new` and `registered` are separate. `registered` needs zone, RDAP or OpenINTEL-confirmed evidence. Every answer states its corpus horizon. | §2 |
| A VPS later | It is one more source that writes the same segments. | §5.7 |
| Many feeds, one reading of our own | Both want THC's dumps, OpenINTEL's Kafka, names tiles, ISC and the NRD lists. | `docs/sources.md` |
| Newness is a definition | Neither design lets "newly registered" be a boolean. | §2, §3.3 |

## 2. Where they split, and what decided it

| Question | Design A | Design B | Decided | What decided it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Keys | The hash of the registrable domain, 4,096 partitions. | Reversed labels (`np.com.example`). | B | A suffix or parent query is one contiguous range, for names and for the feed. Hash partitioning cannot answer "every name under `.np` in a window" without reading all 4,096 partitions. |
| Compression | Left open: "a format the Worker can decode natively", with `DecompressionStream` unmeasured. | `deflate-raw` blocks. At 4,096 names a block the density is 6.04 B a name, and brotli level 9 and zstd level 12 are within 1.5% (design B M10). | B | `DecompressionStream('deflate-raw')` is native in Workers. DOM-008 measures its CPU in workerd. |
| Retention | Names index kept 400 days by last seen. Delete through the GHCR API was an open question. | Keep all while the total is under about 200 GB. Pruning optional. | Keep everything | Ruling 5: "keep raw data always, ghcr is free and no need to delete it". |
| Sources | The THC dumps, Kafka `certstream_domains` and the NRD topics, names tiles, the NRD lists, open ccTLD zones, `sk-nic`, CZDS later. | The same, and ISC `domaindata.json.gz` and WhoisDS. It dropped `cenk/nrd` as stale. | The union | `cenk/nrd` is read from raw GitHub only, because its CDN copy is stale (design A M12). ISC is the best free daily feed (design B M6). |
| What the OpenINTEL NRD stream means | A new-registration claim of tier T2. | "First seen in a certificate", not a registration date. 38% of events carry a certificate older than 30 days. In an RDAP check, 25 of 25 fresh-certificate events were registered within a day and 0 of 25 old-certificate events were new. | B | The RDAP check (design B M5). A certificate older than 7 days marks an event `suspect`. |
| Name-level newness | Label answers `warming` for 90 days and seed a baseline from the Common Crawl domain rank. | Name-level newness is censored until about one certificate validity period of corpus exists. Domain-level newness from feeds with their own history is not. | B, with A's `warming` | A name recurs only at a renewal: 6.84% of 2026-10-26's names were in the day before, and 86.6% were absent from four earlier days (design B M2). The Common Crawl seed is not adopted: no one measured it. |
| Names tiles | Not every log has them. The Kafka stream carries no Let's Encrypt, Geomys or IPng log. THC held 97.7% to 98.1% of the Let's Encrypt tiles' names. | It expected IPng and GoDaddy to have names tiles. | A | Design A's probes (M10). Our own tile reader is the redundant path for Let's Encrypt. Availability is probed on every run. |
| THC's dump | Sorted, as documented. | Not bytewise sorted. A locale collation with 10.3 million adjacent inversions. 0.57% uppercase lines. | B | Design B M2. The ingester normalises and sorts the dump itself. |
| The clock | Actions only. Estimated runner time of 6 to 7 hours a day. | Actions are unreliable (median gap 227 min on a `*/15` schedule). A Cloudflare cron Worker carries freshness and writes a 72 h hot tier to R2. | Measure both (ruling 8) | The operator asked for measurements, with Actions as the default. DOM-001, DOM-002 and DOM-003 decide. |
| Reverse IP and CNAME | Brokered from THC's live API with stale-serve. A THC monthly import was a later phase. | A cached pass-through of THC's live API. It said not to mirror the 53 GB monthly dump. | Neither | Ruling 7: serve both from THC's data mirrored in GHCR, never from THC's live API. |
| A watchdog that starts workflows | Workflows start each other, as in `mirror-archlinux`. | A Cloudflare watchdog dispatches workflows with a fine-grained token held as a Worker secret. | A | Ruling 6: no credential crosses between GitHub and Cloudflare. |
| Packages | Five, to limit the manual "make public" steps. | Four. | Eight, by purpose | Ruling 6: a package needs no manual step. The count is no longer a constraint. |
| The ledger of seen domains | An exact `regs` table. | A Bloom filter in R2, with a daily exact reconcile. | Both | The exact `domains` table is the truth. A filter of about 250 MB lets the hourly job give provisional verdicts. It lives in `state`, not in R2. |
| Response shape | `{data, meta}`. | `{query, coverage, count, next, degraded, domains}`. | A's envelope | One generic shape for every route. `meta` holds the coverage block. |
| Route names | `/v1/new`, `/v1/subdomains/{domain}`, `/v1/ip/{ip}`. | `/domains/new`, `/names?under=`, `/rdns/{ip}`. | A's names and B's `under` parameter | `under` takes any parent, which makes `suffix` a special case. |
| Licence columns | Each source listed its stated terms. | The same, as a field per source. | Dropped | Ruling 4: no licence field and no terms analysis. |

## 3. Corrections both designs made to the older research

The older research is the synthesis and its reports in `web-pkgforge-2026-10-27/`.

| Older claim | Correction | By |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The newly registered topic carries about 239,000 messages a day. | 422,192 in 24 hours by Kafka offsets (design B: 422,534). The earlier figure came from a short sample. | A (M6), B (M4) |
| Zonestream's Kafka broker is on `:9091`. | Port 9091 timed out. The broker answers on plain, anonymous `:9092`. | A (M6), B (M4) |
| Only the WebSocket is available. | Kafka offers replay: about 11.6 days for the NRD topics and about 25 to 27 hours for `certstream_domains`. | A (M6), B (M4) |
| `cs2.ip.thc.org` serves `.txt`. | It serves `<date>.txt.gz` from 2026-10-01. `cs1` answers 302 to `cs2`. | A (M1), B (M1) |
| The daily dump is "one sorted lowercase name per line". | It is not bytewise sorted and carries upper case. | B (M2) |
| IPng and GoDaddy logs have names tiles. | Availability is per log and changes. Gouda2027h1, Halloumi and the GoDaddy tail answered 404 at 05:26Z. | A (M10) |
| `cenk/nrd` is a fresh daily list. | Its CDN copy was five days stale. GitHub's raw copy is current. | A (M12), B (M6) |

## 5. Ideas the record does not adopt

| Idea | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Design A's Common Crawl rank set as a "pre-existing" baseline (135 million domains). | Nobody measured it. `confidence: warming` states the limit honestly. |
| Design A's 4,096-partition hash layout. | See §2, keys. |
| Design A's `kind` tag for machine-generated names with an `exclude=generated` option. | Design B's finding is that 25 registrable domains hold 35% of THC's names. The record keeps them and pages through them. A tag can be added later without a format change. |
| Design B's token for a Cloudflare watchdog. | Ruling 6. |
| Design B's optional plain `names.txt.gz` beside every pack, for the last 60 days only. | The dumps package holds a plain daily dump (DOM-040) for every day, because nothing is deleted. |
