# THC's daily dumps: the adapter, the backfill and what they cost

Research of 2026-11-01 (the design of DOM-029, its measurements and three
challenge lenses) and 2026-11-02 (the four build stages, their benches, their
mutation passes and a dry run of two real days). Written 2026-11-03. The entry
is DOM-029 in `TODO/ingest.md`; the design in force is `docs/architecture.md`
sections 3.4, 4.5 to 4.8, 5.3 to 5.5 and 5.8, and it wins where this file
disagrees.

The question, written before the measurements: can one THC day file, 0.08 to
0.99 GB of gzip, be read from either host, normalised, sorted and written as a
`names` and a `domains` segment that verify, by an adapter that is Worker code
(web APIs only, no child process), inside one runner job of 6 hours and 16 GB?
And what breaks it? The approach is abandoned if a September-size day cannot
fit a job, if the two hosts give other bytes for one day, or if a fault of one
host cannot be read past with the other.

Evidence tags. MEASURED names the instrument or command and the UTC time.
READ names the file. [E] marks an estimate with its basis.

## 0. What this did not establish

Read this before the results.

- **No GitHub runner ran the adapter.** Every number of the adapter's own run
  is from one desktop. A leg's time on a runner is [E]: the desktop's unit
  costs times a runner/desktop ratio of 0.344 or 0.531, two runner runs of one
  day (design M14, M16). The slowest leg of the first backfill measures it.
- **Nothing was pushed or promoted.** The dry run (section 3) ran with
  `--no-push` and no token. No root holds a THC segment, a quarantine field or
  a named hole yet. The root experiment (`19-exp-root.mjs`) ran on synthetic
  segments beside the live root of root_seq 53.
- **The ledger fold, the range compaction and the absorb never ran on real
  packs.** The filter's size after the backfill is `forKeys` arithmetic
  (`16-bench-filter.mjs`, `18-cap-check.mjs`), not a build over 1.5 billion
  records.
- **The desktop was shared and loaded.** Another session worked in the tree,
  this session's own tests and mutation passes ran beside the dry run, and Git
  Bash once printed `fork: retry: Resource temporarily unavailable`. Wall
  times are upper bounds; the CPU times are the better figures. Identical
  bench runs differed by up to 1.7 times within minutes (stage 3).
- **Instruments that are kept but do not reproduce their claim as it ran:**
  - `19-exp-root.mjs` imports `./rp/index.ts`, a scratch copy of
    `packages/root/src` with the first draft's D7 and D8. That copy is not
    kept: the build replaced the first draft's hole rule (the design's
    finding L4), so its E1 to E3 results hold for that draft. The tests of
    `packages/root/test/root.test.ts` hold the built rule.
  - `20-band-trap.mjs` ran against the first draft's band, which held a day
    above the ceiling; the build quarantines it (D26). Its output was not
    kept. `packages/adapter/test/band.test.ts` holds the built verdicts.
  - `08-ratio.mjs` prints the runner cost a name, and its desktop line is
    `NaN` (`08-ratio.rerun.out`). The design's per-line range of 1.88 to
    3.05 µs (M16) was recomputed by the lens in a way that is not kept.
  - `18-cap-check.mjs` failed its first case (`TypeError: reader.readRange is
    not a function`); its second case, the refusal of a 1,881,225,900 B blob,
    ran.
  - The outputs of `05-`, `07-`, `08-` and `21-` with `.rerun.` in their name
    are runs of 2026-11-03 of the copied instrument; the first runs kept no
    output. `05-scan-head.rerun.out` gives the same counts as the design's M11.
- **Revisions.** The design corrected the entry's Approach in 12 places (its
  section 0), and the four build stages corrected the design in at least 7:
  the gap leg that could never end green (F-S4-1), the first live run's day
  (F-S4-2), `runKeys` (S3-D20), the sandbox's `next` run (S3-D5), the budget
  of items 11 and 14 (S3-D18), the harness's deterministic check (S1-D2) and
  the place of `checkResult` (S1-D1). Assume more remain.

**Known-weak claims**, read before the recommendations:

1. The leg times on a runner, 11 to 22 minutes for an October-size day and 35
   to 69 for a September-size day, and the backfill's 4 to 7.2 hours at 6
   legs at once (`07-legs.mjs`), rest on two runner runs of one day and the
   desktop's unit costs of the design stage, not of the built adapter.
2. `runKeys` 500,000 rests on three alternating pairs over 3,000,000 lines
   on the loaded desktop (`26-ab.sh`).
3. The filter after the backfill, about 470 MB after two folds, is [E] from
   the sum of records (M15 plus the October days) and the fold's arithmetic.

## 4. Negative results and routes not taken

- `LC_ALL=C sort -u` in a child process: refused, since an adapter is Worker
  code with no child process.
- One sort of every key: about 22 GB of heap for a September day [E]
  (`01-measure-head.out`), so runs merged by a heap.
- `runKeys` 2,000,000, the design's [E]: slower in each of three pairs and
  more memory (`26-ab.sh`). So the design's lever "raise `runKeys`" for a slow
  leg points the wrong way on this desktop.
- A ceiling of 2.5: a return to September's volume would quarantine two days
  whose new domains are then never new (`06-band-replay-2.5.out`).
- A band that holds a day above its ceiling: a held day writes no history, so
  the baseline never moves and the source holds for good (design M20).
- A leg for a gap day: the adapter's declared gap is a fault, so every
  backfill over 10-04 and 10-09 would end red; the plan writes the gap
  proposal instead (S4-D2).
- A ledger filter sized by the sum of records with no fold: 1.88 GB, over the
  1 GiB a reader takes (`18-cap-check.out`).
- A Range resume of a short body: not built. The second host and the 900 s
  deadline bound the loss to one more read, and none of DOM-009's 54 runner
  reads failed.
